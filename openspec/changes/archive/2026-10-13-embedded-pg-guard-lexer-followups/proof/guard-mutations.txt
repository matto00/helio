# Why the nested-splice variant spans two lines (HEL-1474 cycle 2)
# The direct start is on the same line as the construct. Under the 'nested' mutation the `//` that hides the start also
# comments out the rest of that line, leaving the outer splice open; a one-line form therefore falls back to the RAW scan
# and stays flagged (not failable). The second line `}" // "` closes that splice under the mutation and is balanced under
# the real lexer. It is deliberately not well-formed Scala; only lexical balance matters to the guard.
