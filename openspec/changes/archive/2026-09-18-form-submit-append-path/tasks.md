## Standing Constraints

- [C1] A11y claims are asserted by computed accessible name/description and computed ARIA state (`aria-invalid="true"`, `toHaveAccessibleDescription`, computed live-region role + text, `toBeFocused`), never by DOM/role presence; focus, visibility and announcement claims need Playwright measurement against the running app — jsdom is not evidence.
- [C2] Every `git commit` carries an explicit 600000 ms tool timeout and is never re-run mid-hook; no bespoke poll shells — anchored `pgrep` patterns (`pgrep -c -f '^bash .*<script>'`) or `await-sentinel.sh` only.
- [C3] Never silently drop or coerce a submitted value: every rejected value is surfaced with its field and reason, on the wire and in the UI.
- [C4] If-chain dispatch sites (`PanelContent`, `PanelDetailModal`) are not typecheck-protected — enumerate by hand; this ticket adds no kind, so none change (design.md D11).
- [C5] The merge gate is ruleset `ci-complete` present AND SUCCESS plus `mergeStateStatus` CLEAN; `check-pr-mergeable.sh`'s empty-rollup pass (CON-207) is not the gate; never `gh pr merge --auto`.
- [C6] DESIGN.md is binding for `frontend/**`: tokens only, shared primitives, and visual cohesion compared against the running app in both themes.
- [C7] A guard must be failable by mutation and the recorded proof must be the mutation that exercises that guard: preserved-input and no-write-on-rejection are proven red first and recorded in `mutation-evidence.md`.
- [C8] Write path: a rejected submit writes nothing — proven by a row count before and after, never inferred from a 400 alone; the append target is the panel's persisted `dataSourceId` and nothing else.
