### Verdict: REFUTE

Two defects in the proof plan. Both are cheap to fix. If they stay unfixed, the AC2 and AC3 proofs can come out wrong or meaningless, or the executor has to improvise them.

### Change Requests
1. **D3.2 (task 3.1) contradicts itself as written.** It says "hold `/api/auth/me` for the whole test" and expects green. But the bounce under test needs the `/home` document's own `GET /api/auth/me` to return 401 before it redirects to `/login`. If that call is held for the whole test, the new test hangs at `waitForURL("/login")` and goes red for a reason that has nothing to do with the fix. Rewrite D3.2 as the same release-controlled hold used in D3.1: hold `/api/auth/me` until a test-controlled release placed right after the `setItem`, then let it continue. Make the load-bearing assertion of D3.2 explicit: zero `/api/auth/me` requests are observed between the seed `goto` and the second `goto("/")` in the new shape. That count directly proves AC1 ("seeded with no Helio app code running"). The pass/fail result alone does not.
2. **D3.3 / AC3: the font-held condition is undefined, and the design only considers the seed load.** The second `goto("/")` and `waitForURL("/login")` both wait for `load` by default. As far as I know, a `media="print"` stylesheet still delays the `load` event, and `app.html` has the same non-blocking font link (lines 45-60). So a font stylesheet that hangs forever may stall the `/home` or `/login` documents' `load`, and with it the fixed test. This may well be the ticket's original "intercepted font" failure mode, and it is separate from the hint race. The design must (a) define "held": hung until test end, or delayed by N ms and then released; (b) have the executor determine, by probe, whether `goto("/")`/`waitForURL` depend on the font `load` under that condition; (c) if they do, specify the fix within C2. For example, use `waitUntil: "commit"` / `"domcontentloaded"` on the navigation waits, never a larger timeout. Record the result in `proofs.md`. Without this, AC3 ("no longer depends on stylesheet timing") is unaddressed beyond the seed step.
