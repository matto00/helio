### Non-blocking notes (executor must honour)
- N1 (wiring): D6(b) says the validator runs in `DashboardProposalService.validate` "(propose and PUT contents)". But `DashboardContentsService` (PUT contents) does NOT call `DashboardProposalService.validate`; it calls `ProposalPanelSupport.preValidateBindings` directly (DashboardContentsService l.61), and has its own outputRepo. To make PUT contents truly covered, put the control check in the shared `ProposalPanelSupport` (preValidateBindings or a sibling called by both) rather than only in `DashboardProposalService.validate`. Also `OutputControlsValidator` needs a `NodeSnapshotRepository` (dropdown eligibility); both services currently carry only `outputRepo`, so constructor/wiring changes (and their test fixtures) are needed.
- N2 (D6(d) hedge): a patch-set preview stage exists (`PatchSetPreviewService`, projection only).
