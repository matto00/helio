### Non-blocking notes (executor should act on these)
1. "Every validationError implies a failing run" is NOT true and the artifacts do not verify it. Most analyze validationErrors are derived from the source's inferred_schema ("Unknown field 'x'", PipelineAnalyzeService L665/702/733/755/789/826/885/952; assert rules L1100) and pivot/unpivot/window/fillnull checks that HEL-1267 itself concedes are not in the run-time required-config validator.
