### Non-blocking notes

- Decision 7's generalization of the single-column `filterNot`+`:+` pattern (used today only for one output column at a time in `inferWindow`/`inferStringOps`) to a loop over `Vector[String]` is a reasonable, low-risk generalization, not a new mechanism — implementation should watch for O(n²) `filterNot` scans if `columns` is large, though this is a non-issue at expected reference-table scale and not worth a design-gate objection.
- Decision 4 (first-match-wins) is a real behavioral choice with a documented rationale; worth double-checking at evaluation time that the scaladoc on `LookupStep` states it as prominently as design.md does, since it's the one semantic a future reader could most plausibly want to challenge.
