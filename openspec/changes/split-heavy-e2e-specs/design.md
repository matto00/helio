The ticket's pointer to `.concertino/scratch/HEL-1772/` is stale (swept); the archived change `openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-18-rebalance-e2e-shard-weights/` (design Decisions 4-5) holds the HEL-1772 method and numbers. ## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A named, evidenced variance cause for each of hel1665, hel1733, hel1583 (and confirmation that hel1608 is a stale row, not variance).
- No part of hel1665, hel1733 or hel1583 heavier than about 250 s summed (median) after the change, so none of those three can push a leg by more than about 60 s wall through its own swing. Other heavy files (hel1575-dashboard-tabs about 750 s, hel1582-sweep-375 388-512 s, hel1684-draft-structural, hel1582-mobile-pipeline, state-surface-contrast-guard) are out of scope here; see Decision 1's rule for them.
- Every split file weighted from CI evidence; zero defaulted specs other than ones with no counting main run yet (listed in the PR).
- Slowest leg at or under 1200 s on normal runs; slowest and mean leg reported on at least 5 counting CI attempts with headroom under 1500 s.

**Non-Goals:**
- Raising `timeout-minutes`, changing the leg count, or changing the LPT partition algorithm.
- Fixing flaky tests (C11 list) or the e2e-setup apt mirror timeouts (described in the PR only; see Decision 7).
- Splitting specs other than the three named heavy ones, or editing the in-flight sibling specs (HEL-1781, HEL-1784).
- Changing any test's assertions, title, project, viewport, timeout or fixture.

## Decisions

1. **Variance cause per spec, and what drives each run's slowest leg, from CI evidence only.** Over two populations kept apart, (i) the counting 7-leg main runs since HEL-1772 merged (44446279352, 44455550476, 44468195695, 44488684006, 44498291938, and any later counting ones) and (ii) PR #1172 run 44428229791 attempts 1-6 (attempt 6 from its JSON reports; attempts 1-5 parsed from the job logs' `list` reporter lines, since their reports were replaced by later reruns), compute for **every discovered spec**: (a) summed duration per run, and its share of the run's total summed duration; (b) per-test duration spread (does one test or the whole set swing?); (c) the leg it ran on, the leg's per-worker busy time and the other worker's idle tail (from JSON `workerIndex`/`startTime`; for log-sourced attempts, the per-worker split is not available, so the idle tail is derived from the last test-line timestamp versus the step end where possible and otherwise marked unavailable); (d) the file's in-file mode. A spec whose share of the run total is stable while its absolute sum swings is a **run-wide** cause (runner speed / co-scheduled load: no weight can absorb it, only smaller files can spread it); a spec whose share swings is a **spec-specific** cause, which must be named further (which tests, what they wait on). The result is `variance.md` in this change directory, one section per spec with the numbers and the named cause. Additionally, for every run/attempt in both populations: the slowest leg, its excess over the mean leg (job wall and summed test time), and the specs that account for that excess (spec's summed time in that run minus its median across the population, ranked). Every number carries its source (JSON report or job log). This is done before any spec is split.
   - **Rule for a material driver outside the three named specs** (a spec that accounts for at least 60 s of a slowest leg's excess in two or more runs, or swings by 30 % or more and weighs over 250 s): it is named in `variance.md` with its numbers and becomes a follow-up ticket (filed by the orchestrator at Delivery through the follow-up triage), not split here; the ticket names three specs, and widening that is an owner decision.
   - **Is the three-spec remedy expected to meet AC3?** AC3 asks for slowest and mean leg on at least 5 counting runs with headroom under 1500 s; that is expected to hold (the worst counting main run is 1178 s, 322 s of headroom). The ticket's 1200 s outcome is not guaranteed by this change: on main it already holds on all 5 counting runs, and the one measured near-miss (1161 s) was driven by specs outside the three. Splitting the three still helps every leg, because LPT balances better with finer-grained files and the hel1733 serial tail goes away; but the PR reports any attempt over 1200 s with its driver, and a driver outside scope goes to the follow-up per the rule above. `variance.md` states this expectation again with the full-population numbers, and if those numbers contradict it (e.g. the three named specs drive no slowest-leg excess at all), the executor stops and reports before splitting, so the orchestrator can escalate.
   - Alternative: assume the cause and split. Rejected: the ticket and driver require the cause named first, and a spec-specific cause (e.g. a wait that scales with something) would survive a split.
2. **Remedy: split into balanced files; cut cost only for a named, setup-only waste.** Split hel1665 into 4 files, hel1733 into 3, hel1583 into 2 (by theme), along existing `describe`/loop boundaries, aiming for parts of roughly equal median weight and none above about 250 s. Names: `<original-stem>-<part>.spec.ts` with a short descriptive part (e.g. `hel1583-glass-nav-light.spec.ts`); the original file is removed. Shared constants, types and helpers move verbatim to `e2e/support/<original-stem>.ts` (the existing support directory). Each part repeats the original's file-level `test.describe.configure`, `test.setTimeout` and `test.use` exactly. Every test keeps its exact title path (describe chain + title), project, body and assertions. If the cause analysis names a spec-specific waste in setup (not in an assertion), it may be cut, listed in `variance.md` with per-test CI durations before and the reason it cannot change what is asserted; anything touching an assertion is out of scope.
   - hel1608 is not split: 5 tests, about 100 s summed; its row is regenerated.
   - Alternative: `mode: "parallel"` on hel1733 instead of splitting. Rejected as the primary remedy: it only helps within one leg, does not let LPT place the parts on different legs, and changes in-file isolation assumptions that would need their own proof. Splitting gives both.
   - Alternative: raise `timeout-minutes` or add an 8th leg. Rejected: forbidden by the spec for this requirement / an owner decision not asked for here, and neither fixes a single heavy file landing whole on one leg.
3. **Before/after proof that nothing was lost or weakened.** (a) `npx playwright test --list --reporter=json --project=dev` on the base (3bf3590ec) and on the branch, reduced to `project + title path` per test (file and line stripped): the two lists must be identical as multisets, and the count per project equal. (b) For each moved test, the body text is unchanged: a script extracts each `test(...)` callback body from the old file and the new files (keyed by title path; for template titles, by the template source) and compares them after whitespace normalisation, reporting zero differences; the count of `expect` calls per original file equals the sum over its parts. Both outputs are saved in `.concertino/scratch/HEL-1778/` and summarised in `variance.md` and the PR body.
4. **Weights for split files: attribute results by title path (tool change, red first).** `node scripts/e2e-shard.mjs weights [--legs <n>] [--list <list.json>] <runDir>...`: with `--list` (the JSON of `npx playwright test --list --reporter=json` on the current tree, the same shape `filesFromList` already reads), each report result is attributed per the rules in the spec delta (reported file if it still has that title path; else the single current file that has it; else the reported file if still discovered; ambiguous moved test fails naming it; a result of a deleted file with no match is dropped and named on stderr). Without `--list` behaviour is byte-for-byte unchanged. With `--list`, the table header's `# Regenerate:` line shows the `--list` form so the documented command reproduces the table, and every result re-attributed away from its reported file is listed on stderr. "Title path" = the describe chain plus the test title, excluding the file-level root suite (which Playwright's JSON titles with the file name) and the project. The selftest gains cases for each rule, each shown red first (by mutating the new code) and then green.
   - Alternative: hand-compute the split rows. Rejected: the table is "never hand-edit", and a one-off script is unreviewed.
   - Alternative: weight the split files from the PR's own CI runs. Rejected: the AC says 7-leg main runs, and the PR runs are the measurement set (using them for both would be circular).
5. **Regenerate the table from the 5 most recent counting 7-leg main runs, last.** Counting = every e2e leg succeeded, every leg's `playwright-json-shard-*` present, `unexpected=0` and `flaky=0` (the tool's `validateRunReports`). Run with `--list` from the branch head. The header records the run ids. Done as the final execution step (and redone at Delivery if main has gained counting runs that include specs still defaulted, e.g. hel1786, HEL-1781's or HEL-1784's specs after they merge). Main runs are never rerun by this lane to make them count. Any discovered spec still without a counting main run is listed in the PR as defaulted.
6. **Proof is CI-only, at Delivery, same method as HEL-1772 Decisions 3 and 5.** After the PR's first run: whole-run measurement reruns of the PR head (labelled as measurement in the PR, distinct from the C11 one-rerun flake rule) until 5 counting attempts or 7 attempts in total, never fewer than 3. Because a whole-run rerun replaces the previous attempt's `playwright-json-shard-*` artifacts, each attempt's reports are downloaded (and their presence recorded for the counting rule) before the next measurement rerun is started; if that is missed, the attempt's per-spec numbers fall back to its job logs and are marked so. Per attempt via `/actions/runs/{id}/attempts/{n}/jobs`: slowest leg, mean leg, headroom under 1500 s, and the "Run e2e suite" step separately from setup. Before set: the counting 7-leg main runs from Decision 5 (per-leg median/max, imbalance as the spec defines). If the 7-attempt cap is hit with fewer than 5 counting attempts, the PR says the bar is not met and why.
   - **"e2e shard balance is measured in CI", clause by clause** (splitting and regenerating both change shard assignment, so it applies):
     - *Before set of the 25 most recent counting runs:* only 5 counting 7-leg main runs exist since 33dd755d9; earlier runs have 6 legs, and a per-leg imbalance across a different leg count is not comparable. The before set used is every counting 7-leg main run available at Delivery (at least the 5 above), with the run ids; the PR states the 25-run clause is not met for that reason. For context only, the PR also reports the 25 most recent counting main runs' imbalance with their leg count shown, never mixed into the comparison.
     - *After imbalance at most 15 s:* reported as measured on the counting PR attempts. This line was accepted as unmet by the owner at HEL-1361 (delivery note in the spec); the PR states met or not met with the number, and claims an improvement only if the after imbalance is lower than the 7-leg before imbalance.
     - *Same-window count-based control:* not applicable; every head has used weighted sharding since HEL-1361, so no count-based heads exist (same as HEL-1772 Decision 3a). Stated in the PR.
     - *Run counting rule and per-leg medians/maxima (whole leg and test step), before and after, with run ids:* met by `measure.mjs`. A measurement script `.concertino/scratch/HEL-1778/measure.mjs` is written and run against the before set during execution.
7. **e2e-setup apt mirror timeouts: described, not fixed.** The measurement separates the setup steps from the test step; the PR reports whether apt-step time differs between legs/attempts enough to change leg wall time, and otherwise states it is out of scope (it fails a leg outright rather than slowing it).

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Run-wide slowdown on a whole run (all legs slow) still pushes the slowest leg toward 1200 s] → Smaller files cut the per-leg spread, not the mean; the PR reports each attempt's mean leg so a slow window is told apart from imbalance; no limit raise.
- [Split parts share module-level state the original relied on (e.g. a `let` set in one describe and read in another)] → Task 2 checks for cross-describe state before choosing boundaries; tests in a `parallel`-mode file already could not rely on it.
- [More files means more per-file worker setup] → Playwright reuses workers across files in a leg; the per-file cost is the module import only, measured in the after set.
- [Title-path attribution mis-assigns a renamed test] → Falls back to the reported file; an ambiguous move fails loudly; selftest covers each rule.
- [A test deleted (not moved) whose title path happens to exist in an unrelated current file is attributed to that file] → Most top-level describes carry ticket ids, but not all (hel1665's tests have no describe; titles start "Home load (...)"/"Home at ..."), and no current collision was found. The safeguard that carries the weight is the stderr list of every result re-attributed away from its reported file, which the executor reviews and records; any such attribution only moves a deleted test's seconds.
- [Drivers outside the three specs (hel1582-sweep-375, hel1573-motion-primitives, hel1575-dashboard-tabs) still push a leg over 1200 s] → Named in `variance.md` and the PR with numbers; follow-up ticket per Decision 1; no scope widening here without an owner ruling.
- [Sibling lanes merge new specs mid-run] → They default until a counting main run includes them; Decision 5 regenerates at Delivery when possible.
